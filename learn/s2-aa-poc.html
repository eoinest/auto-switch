Remove all three S2 leads before USB programming. Its 5V/VBUS pin is tied directly to USB power: the holder’s OFF switch alone does not isolate the servo rail.

Measure first.

With the S2 and servo disconnected, measure about 5.0 V between the breadboard rails. Switch off before connecting them. First test with the servo horn unloaded.
