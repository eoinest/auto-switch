Connect Auto Switch

Set Wi-Fi on your S2 Mini over USB.

Before connecting USB: turn battery power OFF and turn the dedicated booster → VBUS isolation switch OFF (or physically disconnect that VBUS wire). Also unplug the servo’s three-wire connector so the control signal cannot power an unpowered servo. Battery OFF alone does not isolate USB power.

Personal Wi-Fi: 8–63 printable ASCII characters.

This changes only Wi-Fi settings. Firmware and calibration stay on the device. When finished, unplug USB before reconnecting the servo and restoring battery power. Passwords are not stored by this browser app.
