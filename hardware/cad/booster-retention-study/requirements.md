# Received XL63070 mount: acceptance criteria

This study responds to the received module shown in IMG_3222.JPG and IMG_3223.JPG. The previous 40 × 36 mm PCB reference was a placeholder and does not match this board. Do not treat old converter clips as fit-verified.

## Required behavior

- Locate the board laterally and longitudinally; retain it vertically without a fixed loose gap.
- Bear on verified bare PCB regions, not on components, terminal solder joints, or selection pads.
- Leave all four electrical terminals accessible after mounting, including underside solder and wire bends.
- Allow removal of a wired module without sliding fragile components along enclosing rails.
- Use the existing carrier floor attachment pattern where feasible: X22, Y22 and Y68 mm (46 mm pitch). Avoid requiring another main-carrier print.
- Separate the assumed PCB substrate thickness from component height and underside protrusions.
- Preserve ventilation and provide wire strain relief that does not load the PCB terminals.
- Minimize new printed pieces and reuse the existing metric fastener assortment.

## Fit evidence still required

The approximate 16 × 30 mm plan size is useful for concepts, not an exact-fit specification. Confirm length, width, bare substrate thickness, underside clearance and the clear contact patches at both short-end centers. Top photographs do not establish underside geometry.

Before a full replacement print, check a small locating/contact test piece on the unpowered module. Check retention without board bending, component contact, pad obstruction or solder-joint loading. Final screw length depends on the measured mounting stack.

## Deliverable boundary

The current deliverables are concept Blender models, a comparison and fit evidence. They are not an approved production STL or a guarantee of physical snugness.
